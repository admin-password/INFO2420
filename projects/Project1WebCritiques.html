Web Site Critiques

Evaluation Questions

  1. What was your first impression of this site?
  2. How does this site organize the flow of information?
  3. Does the site structure make sense or does it confuse you? Why?
  4. Does the site's graphics/layout/multimedia enhance the site or detract from it? Why?
  5. How long did it take for the home page to load? Was the load time reasonable?
  6. Was the text color and size readable? If a background color or image was used, did it enhance or detract from the site?
  7. Did the site have a navigation bar or other navigation tool? Was the navigation well done?
  8. Did the site have a color scheme or look and feel that was repeated on all the pages?
  9. How does the site respond to smaller window sizes (responsive design)?
  10. What does this site offer that was unusual or very well done?

Pitchfork — https://pitchfork.com/thepitch/

It was a very well formatted, yet simplistic site.

It organizes the flow in a tree structure where each link goes to a different page.

The website's structure makes sense.

The way that the website's columns are formatted are off center and the moving gifs bombard the viewer with colorful lights.'

The load time was reasonable but also noticeable, especially when scaling the website size.

The text sizing ranged quite a bit but was readable. The background colors enhanced readability.

The navigation menu was consistant across sizes and easy to use.

The theme was consistant across the website, with black headers and a white background.

As stated above, it scales pretty well but does lag a bit.

The overuse of flashy gifs was a nice touch to the theme of the loudness that music is, but can be somewhat offputting at first.
